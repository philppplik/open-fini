# AI Orchestration

Vibe can split a bigger job (for example a website with logo, photos and copy) into tasks across studios with
`plan_work`. You see the tasks as a checklist; they run in parallel where possible, share names and colors through a
blackboard, check their own output, and a reviewer checks the result. Ask Vibe to cancel or resume a single task.

Studio guides and OpenFini knowledge skills load only when needed. **Help > Ask about OpenFini** opens a chat that
answers questions about the app itself.

Details: [docs/vibe-orchestration.md](https://github.com/philppplik/open-fini/blob/main/docs/vibe-orchestration.md).
