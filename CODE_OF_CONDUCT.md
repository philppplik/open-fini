# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OpenFini to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful, patient and constructive. Assume good intent.
* Give and accept feedback graciously; critique code and ideas, not people.
* Welcome newcomers and help them succeed.
* Credit the work of others and follow the project's [clean-room policy](docs/clean-room.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, or unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
* Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
* Publishing others' private information without permission.
* Submitting code or assets you do not have the right to contribute.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and events) and to people
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code,
and may warn or ban contributors. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (use the subject "Code of Conduct"). Reports are treated
confidentially and reviewed promptly and fairly.
