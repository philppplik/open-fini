## Works in every studio {studios}

About 100 tools reach into Vector, Pixel, Layout, Code, Office and Scene. Right-click AI is there in the canvas, the
code editor and the website designer.

## Bring your own model {models}

OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, xAI, Mistral, Groq, Together, Gemini, local models through Ollama or LM
Studio, or any OpenAI-compatible server. Keys stay on your device, optionally encrypted with a passphrase.

## Modern plumbing {plumbing}

Tool use with permission modes, MCP servers, retrieval over your own documents, skills in the `SKILL.md` style,
custom tools the assistant can write, and image generation and editing.

## Off by default {off}

Nothing is sent anywhere until you switch AI on. An offline Demo assistant shows how it works without a key.
