## Layers and masks {layers}

Pixel layers, groups, layer masks you paint on or build from a selection, blend modes, and adjustments that apply to
one layer or to everything below.

## Select {select}

Marquees, freehand and polygonal lasso, magic wand and color range. Add, subtract, intersect, feather, grow, shrink,
smooth and invert; turn a selection into a layer, a mask or curves.

## Paint and retouch {retouch}

Brush with pressure, hardness, flow, spacing and a stabilizer, pencil, eraser, bucket and gradients. Clone stamp,
healing and spot healing, red-eye removal, smudge, blur, sharpen, dodge, burn and sponge.

## Adjust without losing anything {adjust}

18 adjustments and 22 live filters as non-destructive layers, a RAW-style Develop pipeline and Tone Mapping, plus
auto levels, contrast and color.

## Transform {transform}

Crop with ratios, resize the image or the canvas, Liquify with push, twirl, pinch, bloat and reconstruct, and Image
Trace to turn a picture into vectors.
