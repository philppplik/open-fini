## Timeline {timeline}

Video and audio tracks, a media bin for video, sound and pictures, and a viewer where you move, scale and rotate
layers directly.

## Keyframes {keyframes}

Animate text, shapes and pictures with keyframes, parent layers to each other and add motion blur.

## Effects and transitions {effects}

Every effect, transition and animation preset sits in one searchable Effects browser; drag one onto a clip.

## Export {export}

MP4, WebM, GIF, PNG frames or WAV. A saved scene is an `.ofini` file that carries its media with it.
