## Two ways, one source {source}

CodeMirror 6 with a tab and undo history per file, completions that know your classes, custom properties and files,
Emmet on `Tab`, and find and replace across the project. The files stay the only source of truth: formatting and
comments survive visual edits.

## Visual designer {designer}

A live canvas at desktop, tablet and mobile breakpoints, with a Navigator, an Add panel, Assets, a Console and a
Problems list.

## Class-based styles {styles}

Layout with flex and grid, spacing, size, position, typography, backgrounds and gradients, borders, shadows and
effects, per class and per breakpoint. The `@media` blocks are written for you.

## Templates and frameworks {templates}

Landing page, portfolio, blog, documentation and link-in-bio templates, plus 13 framework starters. Markdown and
LaTeX documents open here too.

## Git and publishing {publish}

Source control, one-click publishing and static-site export as a ZIP, single-file pages or a folder, with
minification and a sitemap. The preview runs in a sandboxed frame that cannot touch the app.
