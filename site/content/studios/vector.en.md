## Draw {draw}

Pen with click-and-drag Béziers, pencil with curve fitting, rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star, line and 17 preset
shapes.

## Edit every node {edit}

Move, convert, break, join, insert and delete nodes, or drag a segment to reshape it. Transform handles work on
rotated objects; snapping follows guides, objects, the grid and margins.

## Combine {combine}

Add, Subtract, Intersect, Exclude and Divide. Convert to curves, expand a stroke, or trace an image into vectors.

## Style {style}

Solid, linear, radial and conical gradients on fills and strokes; stroke alignment, caps, joins and dashes; 16 blend
modes; live shadows, glows, outlines, color overlays and blur.

## Type {type}

Artistic text and text frames with an in-canvas editor that handles input methods, character and paragraph controls,
and your own font files.
