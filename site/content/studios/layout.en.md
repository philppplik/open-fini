## Pages and masters {pages}

Add, duplicate and reorder pages, facing pages, bleed, margins, columns and guides. Master pages carry automatic page
number, page count and date fields.

## Text that flows {text}

Link frames across pages, flow into a new frame or autoflow. Justification and hyphenation, tabs with leaders,
bullets and numbering, drop caps, widow and orphan control, and a table of contents built from heading styles.

## Tables and charts {tables}

Editable tables with formulas, and charts linked to them: change a number and the chart follows.

## Data merge {merge}

Load a CSV file, preview each record in place and export one merged PDF.

## Print-ready output {output}

PDF with embedded TrueType and OpenType fonts, bleed and crop marks. Everything from the Vector studio is available
on the page too.
